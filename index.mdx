This is a gathering place for a very old idea: that God is love, and that love does not fail — that in the end, God will be all in all.

For much of its history, the Christian world has assumed that God's justice ends in eternal, unending torment for most of humanity. That assumption has driven people from the faith, deepened trauma for those who stayed, and told a story about God that scripture, the early church, and reason itself often argue against.

This site exists to make the case — and more than the case, to gather the resources — for a different possibility: universal reconciliation, the ancient and living Christian hope that no one is finally lost.
