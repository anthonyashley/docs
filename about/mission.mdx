## Vision

A world in which Christian universalism is the predominant view for the vast majority of Christians — a world where people who would otherwise be turned away by a faith centered on eternal conscious torment instead find a welcoming home in the belief that God is love, and that God will be all in all.

Eternal conscious torment has done real damage: it has traumatized countless Christians, driven others from the faith entirely, and misrepresented who God is. This project exists to help repair that, in whatever small way it can.

## Mission

To build the internet's most complete hub of resources on apokatastasis — the restoration of all things — including:

- Podcasts and podcast episodes from across the internet
- Patristic texts and academic resources
- Videos, mostly from YouTube
- Essays, blog posts, dissertations, and published reviews
- Book reviews
- Original commentary on the biblical passages central to this discussion

## What this site adds

- **Curation** — pointing to the best existing work rather than duplicating it
- **Context and connection** — helping ideas and resources relate to one another
- **Original content** — commentary and reviews written specifically for this project
- **Guided journeys** — paths through the material based on topic or question, not just a flat archive
